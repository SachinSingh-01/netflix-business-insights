# Netflix Business Insights | Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores the Netflix dataset to uncover trends and business insights related to content distribution, ratings, genres, countries, directors, and audience targeting.

The goal was to understand how Netflix's content library has evolved over time and identify patterns that can support business decisions.

---

## Key Findings

* Movies make up the majority of Netflix's content library.
* Content additions increased significantly after 2015.
* TV-MA is the most common rating, indicating a strong focus on mature audiences.
* The United States contributes the largest share of Netflix content.
* International Movies are among the most popular genres on the platform.
* A small number of directors contribute a large number of titles.
